Privacy Policy

PlateRoom is a gym log that keeps your workouts on your iPhone, and in your account only if you choose to sign in.

Last updated 18 November 2026

What we collect

PlateRoom has no ads and no tracking. You can use it without an account, and then we never receive your workouts. If you choose to sign in, your log is saved to your account, as described under Account (optional). Apart from that, PlateRoom uses Google Firebase for two things:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the app crashes, a report with the stack trace, the app version, the device model, iOS version and free memory at that moment. These help us fix bugs. They are on unless you turn off Settings → Privacy → "Send crash reports".
  • Anonymous usage (Firebase Analytics): counts such as setups finished, workouts started and finished, and sets logged. Only if you choose Share when PlateRoom asks, or turn on Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage".

Neither includes your workouts, exercise names, weights, notes or anything that identifies you. Both use a random identifier for this installation of the app, not your Apple Account or advertising identifier, and are not linked to you, or to your account if you have one, or used for advertising. Google processes this data for us under the Firebase privacy and security terms and keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Data on your device

Your workouts, templates, weekly plan and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you don't sign in, they are stored only there, nothing about your workouts leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes them.

Account (optional)

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with Apple or Google on the last setup page or in Settings → Account, so your log comes back on a new phone or after reinstalling. If you never sign in, none of this section applies.

  • What is saved: your workouts (sets, weights, reps and times), templates, custom exercises, weekly plan and your kg/lb choice. From Apple or Google we get your account's user ID and email address; with Sign in with Apple this may be a private relay address. We don't ask for your name.
  • Where: Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication), stored in the EU (europe-west3, Frankfurt). Google processes this data for us under the Firebase privacy and security terms.
  • Who can read it: only you, when signed in. Firestore security rules allow each account to read and write only its own data, and Firebase App Check only lets the real PlateRoom app reach it.
  • What it's not used for: analytics, advertising or tracking. Crash reports and anonymous usage stay anonymous and are never linked to your account.
  • Signing out: Settings → Account → Sign out stops saving from that iPhone. Your data stays in your account and on the phone.
  • How long and deleting: your data is kept until you delete your account. Settings → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account and everything saved in it, revokes Sign in with Apple, and also deletes all workouts, templates and the plan on that iPhone, so the app starts over. This can't be undone, so export your workouts first if you want a copy.

Import and export

Importing a CSV from Strong or Hevy and exporting a CSV only use files you pick, and only when you ask.

Motion

If you turn on "Shake to log set", PlateRoom reads the motion sensor while a workout is running to notice a shake. Motion data is used on the device at that moment and never stored or shared.

Children

PlateRoom is not directed at children. Without an account it collects no personal information from anyone. Crash reports and anonymous usage contain no personal information. If you sign in, we store only what is listed under Account (optional), and you can delete it at any time.
