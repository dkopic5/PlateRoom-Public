Privacy Policy

PlateRoom is a gym log that keeps your workouts on your iPhone.

Last updated 17 November 2026

What we collect

PlateRoom has no account, no ads and no tracking, and we never receive your workouts. It uses Google Firebase for two things:

  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): when the app crashes, a report with the stack trace, the app version, the device model, iOS version and free memory at that moment. These help us fix bugs. They are on unless you turn off Settings → Privacy → "Send crash reports".
  • Anonymous usage (Firebase Analytics): counts such as setups finished, workouts started and finished, and sets logged. Only if you choose Share when PlateRoom asks, or turn on Settings → Privacy → "Share anonymous usage".

Neither includes your workouts, exercise names, weights, notes or anything that identifies you. Both use a random identifier for this installation of the app, not your Apple Account or advertising identifier, and are not linked to you or used for advertising. Google processes this data for us under the Firebase privacy and security terms and keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Data on your device

Your workouts, templates, weekly plan and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them, unless you have made a backup.

Backups, import and export

If you turn on backups, PlateRoom writes a backup file to a folder you choose in the Files app, such as iCloud Drive or On My iPhone. If that folder is in iCloud Drive, Apple stores the file under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy applies. Importing a CSV from Strong or Hevy and exporting a CSV only use files you pick, and only when you ask.

Motion

If you turn on "Shake to log set", PlateRoom reads the motion sensor while a workout is running to notice a shake. Motion data is used on the device at that moment and never stored or shared.

Children

PlateRoom does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Crash reports and anonymous usage contain no personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
