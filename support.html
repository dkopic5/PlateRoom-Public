PlateRoom Support

A free, fast gym log. One tap per set.

Get help

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. If you're testing on TestFlight, you can also shake your phone or use the TestFlight app to send feedback with a screenshot.

How do I change my training week?

Settings → "Set up my week again" runs the setup again. You can also edit any workout and the weekly plan on the Templates tab.

How do I keep my data if I delete the app or get a new phone?

Sign in with Apple or Google in Settings → Account (or on the last setup page). New workouts and changes are then saved to your account automatically, and they come back when you sign in again on a new phone or after reinstalling. Without an account, your log is only on that iPhone. Signing in is optional; see the Privacy Policy for what is saved and where.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved in it, and also the workouts, templates and plan on that iPhone. If you want a copy, use Settings → Export first. To stop saving without deleting anything, tap Sign out instead.

Can I bring my history from another app?

Yes. Settings → Import from another app (Strong or Hevy). Export a CSV from that app first; the import screen shows how.

How do I log a set without touching the screen?

Turn on Settings → Hands-free → "Shake to log set", or set up the Action Button or Back Tap with the PlateRoom shortcuts. Every hands-free log can be undone.
