PlateRoom is a fast, free gym log for iPhone, built for the moment between sets. No account needed, no ads.

Build your week in a few taps

Pick your training days, a ready-made split or one of your own, the gear you have and how long you train. PlateRoom builds your workouts and your weekly plan.

Log fast

  • One tap logs a set with last session's numbers filled in
  • The rest timer starts by itself
  • Shake, the Action Button or Back Tap when your hands are full

On your Lock Screen

Your workout stays on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island: the next set, the rest countdown, and Log set, +15 s and Skip rest without unlocking.

Change it for this week

Tap today's workout to swap, add or reorder exercises just for this week. Your template stays as it is.

See your progress

New personal records are called out the moment you hit them. History has a calendar, streaks, weekly volume and strength charts.

Your data stays yours

No ads, no tracking. Your workouts stay on your iPhone unless you choose to sign in with Apple or Google to keep them safe in your account. Import from Strong or Hevy, and export to CSV any time.
